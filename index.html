Beat Beasts

Giant beasts are tearing up the city, and every one of them is stuck in a feeling. You're the Crew Captain. Match their mood with music, play the beat that brings them back, then help them find their place in your crew.

  1. Read the scouting report. Pick the riff that sounds like how the beast feels.
  2. Hit the notes on the beat. Keys or the big pads both work.
  3. The music slowly shifts. Keep the beat and the beast shifts with it.
  4. Talk to it. Listen, find out what's really going on, and help it find its place in your crew.
